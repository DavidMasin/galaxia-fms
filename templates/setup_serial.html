Serial Devices

Boards with no network connect to the FMS over a USB serial port, at 115200 baud. Tick a port to keep it open for a device; a port that is unplugged is opened again by itself when it is back. A device on a serial port needs no token, but it still has to be approved on the Devices page the first time.

The serial transport is not running yet: chosen ports are opened once the FMS is.
